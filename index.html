Instagram automation

WhatsApp automation

Lead tracking

Every enquiry lands in one sheet with its source, so you can see which reel brought which admission.

Consent first

We collect phone numbers only with the student's or parent's consent, and follow WhatsApp's opt-in rules.

For Lalit Tutorials

Our first 30 days.

Based on what we saw on your Instagram, website and public presence. These are ideas for discussion, not official Lalit Tutorials material.

01

Fix the basics

  • Clear Instagram bio, pinned posts and a WhatsApp enquiry button.
  • One phone number everywhere. The website currently shows two.
  • Check spellings on the site and chatbot.
  • Posting calendar and a pre-publish checklist.
02

Build trust

  • Teacher intro reels, so parents meet the faces behind the results.
  • Student journeys, with parent consent.
  • Free "doubt of the day" and study-tip reels.
  • Behind the scenes at the centre.
03

Turn views into admissions

  • One clear action on every post: WhatsApp, call or form.
  • Track enquiries by source each week.
  • Promote scholarship tests (LTSE, FEST) with simple local ads.
  • Results-day templates ready, with a second person checking ranks and names.
WEEK 1Clean-up and intro

Profile refresh · "Meet the teachers" reel · pinned admission post

WEEK 2Free value

3 study-tip reels · "Doubt of the day" series · NEET/JEE myths carousel

WEEK 3Student stories

Two student journeys (with consent) · classroom behind the scenes · parent testimonial

WEEK 4Admissions push

Scholarship test countdown · course comparison carousel · "How to enquire" reel · weekly report

What we'd measure: reach, saves and shares, profile visits, WhatsApp enquiries, counselling calls and admissions, reported weekly in plain language. Safeguards: written parent consent for any student under 18, and a spelling and rank check on every result post.

How we work

Two people, one workflow.

We split the work between us: concepts, scripts and writing, shooting, editing, and posting. Each of us can cover the other, so content never stops.

Plan

Monthly calendar with content pillars: results, teachers, students, free value, announcements.

Create

Concept, script, shoot, edit and design in-house, using templates so the brand looks consistent.

Review

Check spelling, facts and consent, then publish at the right time.

Respond

Reply to comments and DMs quickly. Move serious enquiries to WhatsApp or a call.

Learn

Look at what worked each week and repeat it.
